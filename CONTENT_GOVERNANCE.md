# Biswas Exports Content Governance

This document records the evidence standard used for public website content.

## Confirmed public information

- Public-facing brand: **Biswas Exports**
- Operating location presented on the website: **Asansol, West Bengal, India**
- Catalogue scope: **87 indicative B2B sourcing categories**
- Catalogue images: **AI-generated illustrative visuals**

## Information not to publish until verified

Do not publish or imply any of the following without current documentary evidence and owner approval:

- Legal entity or proprietorship name linked to Biswas Exports
- GSTIN, IEC, PAN, registration number or establishment year
- Full street address
- Public business email, phone or WhatsApp number
- Manufacturer, supplier, agent, distributor or overseas-office appointments
- Existing stock, warehouse inventory or confirmed prices
- Certifications, licences, registrations, laboratory reports or memberships
- Completed exports, shipment volumes, buyer names, countries served or testimonials
- Guaranteed quality, performance, delivery, regulatory approval or market acceptance

## Catalogue rules

1. A product listing is an **indicative sourcing category**, not an offer or stock declaration.
2. Product origin, supplier identity, availability, specification, MOQ, packaging, documentation and price must be reconfirmed before quotation.
3. Images must be described as illustrative and must not be presented as actual inventory, packaging, supplier facilities or completed shipments.
4. Organic, vegan, plant-based, eco-friendly, sustainable, sterile, surgical, medical, food-grade and similar claims require product-specific evidence.
5. Medicines, medical devices, chemicals, batteries, food, cosmetics, electrical goods and toys require destination-specific regulatory review.
6. No therapeutic, medical, legal, compliance or performance guarantee may be made through catalogue copy.

## Service-copy rules

- Describe activities as review, coordination or support unless a contractual responsibility has been accepted.
- Do not imply that Biswas Exports manufactures, certifies, tests, transports or approves a product unless that role is documented.
- Third-party suppliers, laboratories, inspection bodies and logistics providers remain responsible for their own services.
